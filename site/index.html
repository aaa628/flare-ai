The Flare AI method, in three steps

  1. Copies NASA’s numbers

    Each record comes from a NASA report table or data file, in NASA’s own units. Nothing is rounded, merged or filled in.

  2. Checks each record against its source

    126 BASS-II records match NASA’s PDF value for value. No person has signed one yet, so none is called verified.

  3. Stops where the data stops

    Ask about conditions NASA never tested and the answer is “insufficient data”, with the nearest real tests listed.

Where does Flare AI’s data come from?

From NASA’s BASS-II summary report and NASA’s Physical Sciences Informatics database. Each record links to its page or file.

Has a person verified the data?

Not yet. Every record is checked by machine against NASA’s files, and none is signed by a person.

Does Flare AI predict fires?

No. It gives no fire probabilities and certifies nothing. It shows what NASA tested and recorded.

Who built Flare AI?

Studio 628, a student team, for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026. NASA does not endorse this project.
