## NASA Data & Resources
[https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=combustion] 
PSI Data Repository: Combustion Science
:
 Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) source of the data upon which the NASA Space App is to be based.
[https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/glenn-expertise-space-exploration/physical-sciences-program/combustion-science/] 
NASA Combustion Science
:
 Basic information about most of the combustion investigations for which data is found in the PSI Data Repository.
[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1oXykoz9zg] 
NASA ScienceCasts: The Power of Studying Combustion on the ISS
:
 Introduction to NASA’s combustion research on the International Space Station through a 3.6-minute video clip from 2018.
[https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/a-researchers-guide-to-combustion-science/] 
A Researcher’s Guide to International Space Station Combustion Science
:
 A 2015 review of NASA’s combustion research conducted on, or planned for, the International Space Station. Basic information about most of the combustion investigations described in this 50+ page document is found within the PSI Data Repository.
[https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-1] 
Apollo 1
:
 Information about the crew mission, fire that erupted during a pre-launch test killing the crew, investigation, and lessons learned.
[https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-13] 
Apollo 13
:
 Information about the crew, mission, oxygen tank explosion that occurred during the flight to the Moon, investigation, and lessons learned.
[https://www.nasa.gov/history/25-years-ago-fire-aboard-space-station-mir/] 
25 Years Ago: Fire Aboard Space Station Mir
:
 Basic information about a fire—caused by an oxygen generator failure—that erupted within the Russian space station Mir in 1997 during joint operations in preparation for the International Space Station (first occupied in 2000).
[https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9582] 
Fireman’s Air Tanks
:
 Basic information about the 1970s adaptation of spacesuit technology to improve firefighters’ air tanks and breathing gear.
[https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9407] 
Fire Detector
:
 Basic information about a 1970s smoke detector developed for the shuttle that, discriminating between smoke and dust, results in less nuisance alarms. As such, it has industrial applications (e.g., in mining operations).
[https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9918] 
New Technology for the Fire Attire
:
 Basic information about a 1980s application of NASA spacesuit technology to improve firefighter clothing.
